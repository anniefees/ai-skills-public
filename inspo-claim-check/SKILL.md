# Inspo claim check

## Steps
1. Watch and transcribe the reel. If you cannot access it, ask for the file. Never guess the claims.
2. Find the product or public repo it's about.
3. Check real capabilities, cost, setup, and fresh stats (re-fetch, cached numbers go stale).
4. Give a verdict: true or oversold.
5. If they want a next step, offer a short summary they can reuse (what is true, what is oversold, and what to say). Do not create records or posts unless they ask.
