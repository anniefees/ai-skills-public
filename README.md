# AI skills by Annie Fees

Free skills you can drop into your own AI bot.

This index lists every skill in the repo. The Instacart grocery skill is live. The other skills are drafts, so check the work before you rely on them.

## Content and video

- [brain-dump-to-script](brain-dump-to-script/) turns a series or format plus a brain dump into one cohesive, ready-to-say script in the user's own voice. Use it when you want that single script from a dump.
- [inspo-reel-technique-breakdown](inspo-reel-technique-breakdown/) breaks down the editing techniques in a reel shared as an example. Use it when you want those techniques named so you can reuse the method.
- [script-and-video-feedback](script-and-video-feedback/) gives feedback on a script, take, or cut. Use it when a creator asks what you think of that piece of work.
- [instagram-profile-audit](instagram-profile-audit/) audits an Instagram profile across the bio, grid, hooks, calls to action, and content lanes, and lists the top fixes. Use it when you want that review of a profile.
- [inspo-claim-check](inspo-claim-check/) checks whether the claims in a tool or product reel are true. Use it when someone sends you that reel and asks you to verify it.

## Outreach and sales

- [cold-outreach-playbook-and-templates](cold-outreach-playbook-and-templates/) designs a cold email plus LinkedIn sequence and all of its templates. Use it when you are building that sequence and need every message written out.
- [outbound-volume-deliverability-plan](outbound-volume-deliverability-plan/) plans cold email sending volume and deliverability. Use it when you are deciding how much to send and how to keep those messages deliverable.

## Productivity and automation

- [ai-app-builder-slices](ai-app-builder-slices/) builds or fixes a web app with an AI app builder in small, tested slices with checkpoints. Use it when each change should be checked before the next one starts.
- [no-secrets-in-git](no-secrets-in-git/) keeps API keys and other credentials out of git. Use it when committing, opening a pull request, or handling keys, so secret values stay out of the repository.
- [downloads-cleanup](downloads-cleanup/) cleans up a Downloads folder by listing files, grouping them, and showing the space saved. Use it when the folder is cluttered, and delete only after you say OK.
- [job-posting-watch](job-posting-watch/) watches an employer's careers page for a role and pings only on new openings. Use it when you want alerts for new postings and quiet when nothing new is there.

## Learning

- [licensing-exam-flashcard-deck](licensing-exam-flashcard-deck/) builds fact-checked flashcard decks from a licensing exam study guide. Use it when someone is studying for that exam and wants cards made from the guide.
- [study-guide-to-audio-lessons](study-guide-to-audio-lessons/) turns a written study guide into detailed audio lessons with free local text to speech. Use it when you want to listen through the guide.

## Shopping and money

- [Instacart Grocery Shopper](instacart-grocery-shopper/) builds your weekly grocery cart on Instacart from your Notion preferences, stays within budget, and waits for your OK before checkout. Use it when you want that cart prepared and held until you decide to place the order.
- [product-price-watch](product-price-watch/) watches a product's price at a few stores and pings only when something changes. Use it when you want an alert for a real price change.
- [trial-decision-reminder](trial-decision-reminder/) sets a one-time keep or cancel reminder before a trial or subscription renews. Use it when a renewal is coming up and you want a single prompt to decide.
- [vet-make-money-opportunity](vet-make-money-opportunity/) vets a make-money pitch for whether it is legitimate and worth it. Use it when someone sends you that pitch and asks you to check it.
