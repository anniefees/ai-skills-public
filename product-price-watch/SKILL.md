# Product price watch

## Steps
1. Check the stores the user named for every size of the product they asked you to watch.
2. Check for a gift with purchase or a site-wide sale.
3. Compare with last time. Ping only when something is new.

## Rules
- Quiet when nothing changed. Never quote a discount you didn't see. Never buy without an explicit yes.
