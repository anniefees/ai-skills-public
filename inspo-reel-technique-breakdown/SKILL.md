# Inspo reel technique breakdown

Break down a shared reel into editing techniques you can reuse, without copying its look.

## Steps
1. Download or otherwise get a copy of the reel. If you cannot access it, ask for the file. Never guess the content.
2. Break down shot length, text style, punch-ins, sound effects and music, stickers, and B-roll.
3. Rank the techniques by how easy they are to automate.

## Rules
- Use it as a technique reference only, never a look to copy.
