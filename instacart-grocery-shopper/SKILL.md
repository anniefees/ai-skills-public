# Instacart Grocery Shopper

1. Read my Notion page "Grocery Brain Dump" first: weekly budget, store, staples, protein goals, preferred brands, and no-go items.
2. Open Instacart, pick my store (for example H-E-B), and build this week's cart from my staples list plus a few protein-focused meals.
3. Stay under my weekly budget, including fees and tip. If the cart runs over, cut the extras first, never the staples.
4. Use my preferred brands when they are in stock. Never add anything on my no-go list, and check product names for hidden conflicts (like a "spicy" version of something I'm fine with).
5. If an item is out of stock, swap it for the closest match (same size, similar price) and set the Instacart replacement preference to "Best match".
6. Add anything I ask for in chat this week, like "also grab oat milk and a dozen eggs", on top of the usual list. If a request overlaps a staple, ask me how many I want.
7. Do NOT check out or pay unless I say "place it". When the cart is ready, send me a short summary: items, total, and any swaps.
8. Save what I keep or remove to my Notion page so next week's cart is even better.

## Safety

- If Instacart asks me to sign in, verify a code, or solve a captcha, hand the screen to me instead of guessing.
- Never type my password or card details from chat. Use the payment method I saved in Instacart myself.
