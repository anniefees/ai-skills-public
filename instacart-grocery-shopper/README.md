# Instacart Grocery Shopper

An AI bot skill that builds your weekly grocery cart for you, right up to checkout, and waits for your OK.

## How to set it up

1. Make a new bot in Grok Bot.
2. Paste the contents of [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) into the chat and send it.
3. Connect your Notion account.
4. Copy the free [Grocery Brain Dump Notion template](https://workable-hourglass-0b9.notion.site/Grocery-Brain-Dump-409947125a768195b1b7cb5b740a2c14) into your Notion (click "Duplicate" at the top right) and fill it out: budget, store and delivery, allergies, likes and dislikes, staples, and your checkout rules. In Checkout rules, pick "Ask me first" if you want the bot to check with you on swaps and judgment calls, or "Just run" if you want it to handle them and tell you what it did.
5. Open your bot's computer, log in to Instacart, and add a payment method. A separate virtual card is a good idea, just to be safe.
6. Let it run. Got a special request this week? Just tell your bot in chat.

Your bot never checks out until you say "place it".
