# Vet make money opportunity

## Steps
1. Download the video and caption, transcribe it, and grab on-screen text. If you cannot access it, ask for the file. Never guess the pitch.
2. Identify the method, who's pitching it, and whether it's sponsored or a lead-capture funnel.
3. Research eligibility, startup and ongoing costs, time to first payout, real payout and success rates, with cited sources. Say when data is missing.
4. Fact-check any income screenshots.
5. Judge the fit against the person's goals, time, and current projects.

## Output
Short verdict first, then time, investment, odds, realistic earnings, fit, and source links.

## Rules
- Be honest even when the answer is no. Never invent numbers.
