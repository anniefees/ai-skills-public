# Script and video feedback

## Steps
1. If more than one version is in play, confirm which one to review. Use the newest version (latest take or export, not an old transcript).
2. Reply brief and encouraging: what's working, 1 or 2 fixes, and whether it's ready.

## Rules
- No long structural critiques.
- Don't present your own past suggestions as the creator's rules.
