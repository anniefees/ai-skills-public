# Downloads cleanup

## Steps
1. List the Downloads folder with sizes and dates.
2. Group installers and old draft copies. Keep anything from the last two weeks.
3. Show the list and the total space to free.
4. Delete only after an explicit OK, then confirm the free space.
5. A weekly version still asks every time.
