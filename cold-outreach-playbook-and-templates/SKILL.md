# Cold outreach playbook and templates

## Steps
1. Capture the offer in the user's own words.
2. Lead with a freebie and a permission-based ask.
3. Sequence: Email 1 Day 0, Email 2 Day 3 (same thread), follow-up Day 7, follow-up Day 14, monthly nurture up to 6, then a breakup.
4. Reply-type templates: not now, wrong person, referral, price, what is this, unsubscribe (sends nothing), out of office.
5. Freebie delivery email, call script, meeting confirm, reminder, and recap.
6. LinkedIn connect (no note) plus a DM after accept.
7. Each template gets a trigger, subject, variables, and what the AI personalizes.
8. User choices become per-user settings with conditional variables, never hard-coded.

## Rules
- Plain text. No links in Email 1. Tracking off.
- Nurture acknowledges earlier outreach and restates the value.
- The LinkedIn DM mentions the earlier email and ends with an easy question.
- Unproven claims need proof before use. No fake data; label examples.
- Never send outreach without the user's explicit yes.
